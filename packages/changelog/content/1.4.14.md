## Meet Mentari

- Continue with the newly renamed **Mentari** app, including refreshed desktop
  branding, icons, loading screens, and system integration
- Start new notes with a **Doctor's Visit** template designed for clinical
  conversations without replacing templates you already customized
- Keep sensitive conversations local by default while choosing approved AI
  providers when you want additional intelligence

## Clearer clinical notes

- Hover over a summary line to see the exact transcript passages that support
  it
- Use **Understand This** on medical terms to get a patient-friendly
  explanation connected to the original conversation
- Pull medications, labs, imaging, referrals, follow-ups, and warning signs
  into clear next steps
- See more useful feedback when summary generation cannot continue, including
  sign-in, provider, and configuration problems

## Recording and transcripts

- Show or hide the live transcript directly from the recording toolbar
- Keep transcript placeholders visible with a roomier live transcript panel
- Separate microphone and system-audio channels into speakers when full
  diarization is unavailable
- Reduce speaker-label flicker during short back-and-forth conversations
- Avoid a background process that could close other meeting applications

## Privacy and reliability

- Require device authentication when using **Lock app**, and explain when the
  device cannot provide biometric authentication
- Recover when biometric availability changes while Mentari is already open
- Preserve existing customized templates during the Doctor's Visit template
  migration
- Surface database startup problems instead of leaving the app stuck without
  an explanation
